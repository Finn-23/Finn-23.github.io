Understanding Libraries as Dynamic Space

This site explores the library as more than a place to study. It is a dynamic environment shaped by the people who move through it. Through maps, images, and analysis, this site reveals how patterns of behavior interact to form distinct “habitats” within the library. Rather than focusing on resources alone, this site examines the relationship between students and their surroundings, showing how each continuously influences the other.

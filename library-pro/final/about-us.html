Editorial Team

Editor-in-Chief

Cubert Kyphosis founded the Library Ecologists project after spending years both as a patron of libraries and an observer of the patterns of behavior within them. He now serves as our Editor-in-Chief. His previous research focused on territoral seating behavior and migratory study habits during examination periods.

Journalists

Comfort Expert

Nermal Scolicies serves as the project's comfort expert. He specializes in the identification and evaluation of optimal resting habitats in libraries. With weeks of field experience locating beanbags, couches, and quiet rooms, he is quickly developing an unmatched understanding of how light, furniture, and temperature influence student comfort. His research is a welcome contribution to our ongoing documentation of restorative spaces across libraries.

Wandering Student

James Smith serves as our reporter abroad. From Abu Dhabi to Zurich, he aims to discover all the study spaces the world has to offer. Through field reports and observational research, his work explores how different architecture, customs, and tradtions shape the habits of students across different countries

Ecology Department

Head Ecologist

Cirrus Taenia serves as the head of our ecology department. He oversees the scientific and theoretical foundations of all of the research. He ensures that all published articles are informed by current ecological studies and integrate their theories. His past research focused on bridging the gap between traditional ecology and the study of urban environments

Our Journey

March 13th, 2017

First Recorded Observation

Cubert Kyphosis conducted a preliminary field study after noticing recurring territorial disputes over window seatings during midterm season at a local library.

Library Ecologist Founded

Cubert Kyphosis founded the Library Ecologist project as an interdisciplinary effort dedicated to studying academic ecosystems. Born out of his love for libraries, we have been studying and supporting them ever since!

October 17th, 2017
January 12th, 2018

First Article Published

We published our first article after conducting a study on the impact of lighting on student choice of seating.

Discovery of the Quiet Floor Migration Pattern

After a semester long observation, we became the first to document "migration pattern" among students. We found that students relocated en masse from noiser, more collaborative floors, to silent study floors during midterms and finals week

July 22th, 2019
August 15th, 2020

Named Leading Resource for Sanitary Library Practices

During the COVID-19 Pandemic, we were named by the CDC as a leading resource in how to keep library spaces healthy and clean for those wishing to return back to libraries.

5 year celebration

We celebrated 5 years of library study with a 48-hour library studying marathon.
